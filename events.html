Salve Civic Education Institute

Tuesday, August 18 • 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM • The Pell Center, 518 Bellevue Ave, Newport, RI

A one-day FREE professional development for middle and high school social studies teachers. Participants will explore practical, engaging ways to introduce students to democratic processes, constitutional principles, and public problem-solving.

Sessions include workshops with Dr. Kimberly Behan, Dr. Tracy Pelkowski, Dr. Mary Anderson, Gene Thompson-Grove (League of Women Voters), and Generation Citizen. Co-sponsored by LWVRI.

Limited to 12–16 participants. Administrators and department chairs are also welcome!
