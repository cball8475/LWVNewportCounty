Salve Civic Education Institute

A one-day FREE professional development for middle and high school social studies teachers, exploring engaging ways to introduce students to democratic processes, constitutional principles, and public problem-solving. Co-sponsored by LWVRI.

Apr
23

Discussion on Election Process & Security in Rhode Island

Thursday, April 23 • 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM • Innovate Newport, 513 Broadway, Newport RI

Join the League for a community conversation with Secretary of State Gregg Amore and Deputy Secretary of State Rob Rock on how Rhode Island elections are conducted, current security measures, and key legislative developments including the SAVE Act and the Rhode Island Voting Rights Act, followed by an open Q&A. The Newport County Board of Canvassers will be recognized for their essential work supporting elections in the region.
